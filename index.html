MTA helps you answer four key questions:

  • Where does my money go?
  • How does it move?
  • Where is it now?
  • What commitments do I currently have?

We believe the travel metaphor fits money well: it moves, sometimes returns, and flows between different origins and destinations.

Its main goal is to make your commitments visible and help prevent unpleasant surprises.

You don’t need an account. Your data stays on your device — only you can access it.

MTA is still evolving. You may encounter small issues or missing features — your feedback helps improve the experience.
