# Money Travel Agency (MTA)

A personal finance tracker that answers four key questions:

- Where did my money come from?
- How did it travel?
- Where is it now?
- What commitments do I currently have?

MTA uses a travel metaphor: money moves between airports (categories), terminals (accounts), and travels like flights between destinations.

## Install on iOS

1. Open this link in Safari: `https://gucapote.github.io/money-travel-agency-ios/`
2. Tap the **Share** button (↗️)
3. Select **Add to Home Screen**
4. The app appears on your home screen — tap it anytime to use MTA offline

Your data is stored locally on your device. You don't need an account or internet connection after installation.

## About MTA

- **Fully offline** — All data stays on your device
- **No account required** — Privacy-first design
- **Bilingual** — English and Spanish support
- **Deterministic** — Same results every action

Start by exploring the **Learn** tab to understand the core concepts.
